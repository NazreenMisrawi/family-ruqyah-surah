Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

What this app stores

Daily Ruqyah and Surah stores only the following information in your browser’s local storage:

  • The current values of the recitation counters
  • The date of the last counter reset

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent to any server.

Audio files

The audio files are loaded from a public Cloudflare R2 bucket. No personal information is transmitted when the audio is requested.

No accounts or tracking

The app does not require an account, does not use cookies for tracking, and does not collect analytics.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy notice, contact the maintainer of the repository.
